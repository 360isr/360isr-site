KSA · RECOMMENDED READING

Bibliography

Curated reading list maintained by 360iSR. Books, journal articles, and monographs that inform the doctrine, methodology and operational philosophy of the ISR-T curriculum. New entries are added as they appear and prove their worth.

Doctrine and ISR Theory

David Patrikarakos — War is 140 Characters 2017

How social media shapes modern conflict and the intelligence picture. Cited directly in the Foundation course.

Rupert Smith — The Utility of Force 2005

Foundational reading on the changing character of war and what it demands of intelligence and operations.

Donald Kagan — On the Origins of War and the Preservation of Peace 1995

Strategic-level reading for senior practitioners.

Brown (Joint Force Quarterly vol. 72) — Reframing ISR 2014

Argues for problem-centric ISR over collection-centric. One of the most-cited papers in the 360iSR curriculum.

Lawrence Freedman — Strategy: A History 2013

Essential context for understanding how ISR fits within broader strategic thought.

Decision-Making and Cognition

Daniel Kahneman — Thinking, Fast and Slow 2011

Foundational on cognitive bias. Essential reading for ISR practitioners.

Philip Tetlock — Expert Political Judgement 2005

Why experts get it wrong; the basis of structured analytic techniques.

Philip Tetlock and Dan Gardner — Superforecasting 2015

Practical techniques for better intelligence assessments.

Nate Silver — The Signal and the Noise 2012

On separating prediction from noise in complex systems.

Gary Klein — Sources of Power 1998

Naturalistic decision-making — how experts decide under pressure.

Cognitive and Information Warfare

Thomas Rid — Active Measures 2020

A century of political warfare; essential context for cognitive warfare.

P.W. Singer and Emerson Brooking — LikeWar 2018

How social media has been weaponised in conflict.

NATO Allied Command Transformation — Cognitive Warfare Concept 2021

Foundational NATO position paper on cognitive warfare.

Peter Pomerantsev — This Is Not Propaganda 2019

Disinformation as a tool of statecraft.

Multi-Domain Operations

Christian Brose — The Kill Chain 2020

On the future of US military technology and decision-cycle compression.

H.R. McMaster — Battlegrounds 2020

The strategic landscape facing Western militaries.

Russia–Ukraine — Modern ISR Lessons

Serhii Plokhy — The Russo-Ukrainian War: The Return of History 2023

Historical context for the current conflict and its ISR lessons.

CSIS — Tactical Developments — Third Year of the Russo-Ukrainian War 2025

Open-source assessment with direct ISR implications.
