About · Founder-led, practitioner-grounded

Understanding is a discipline.
Tempo is its measure.

360iSR exists to close the gap between what military forces can now collect and what they can usefully decide. We are a UK-based defence consultancy serving NATO, the Royal Netherlands MoD, the UK Royal Air Force and partner-nation air forces.

01 / Origin

Why 360iSR exists.

The modern force can see more than it can understand, and understand more than it can decide on. ISR architectures have grown faster than the staff design, doctrine and training needed to convert collection into command.

360iSR was founded to operate in that gap. We build the methodology, the doctrine, the training, and the tooling that turns intelligence capacity into decision capacity. Every output is judged on whether it improves the commander’s ability to decide and act, at tempo, under uncertainty.

We are deliberately founder-led and practitioner-grounded. We do not staff with generalist consultants; we operate with serving and former NATO and partner-nation practitioners and bring them to bear on the specific decision problem in front of the client.

02 / Founder

Ewen Stockbridge Sime.

Founder & Director, 360iSR Ltd.

Senior defence and intelligence professional leading development of ISR training platforms and doctrine for military organisations. Founder and leader of 360iSR, a UK-based consultancy specialising in ISR training, targeting support, and decision-architecture frameworks for NATO and allied forces.

Expertise spans NATO procedures (AJP-2, AJP-2.7, AJP-3.9, TLP and the AIntP series), defence procurement governance, strategic communications, and the design of decision-support workflows for joint and combined ISR environments.

Bilingual English and Dutch. Based in London. Maintains active practitioner networks across NATO, the UK Ministry of Defence, and partner-nation intelligence and ISR organisations.
